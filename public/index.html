About

Software engineer at Ophelos, a UK fintech in the Intrum group with an AI-native debt-resolution platform. I work across the Python platform and the Rails monolith it replaces. For two years I have owned client data ingestion, from how a client's file becomes data the platform can act on through to a reconciled ledger, along with the reliability, observability and money-correctness work around it.

This year that has included making an LLM agent's output safe to save, shipping a voice-automation pilot end to end, and taking new clients live with the market's operations team. Earlier at Ophelos I led engineering for a European market launch and rebuilt background-job monitoring. I build with AI coding agents, from plans I write and review.

I have nine years across fintech, startups and enterprise software in the UK and Japan. Before Ophelos I spent six years in Tokyo, leading development of two products at a startup and building banking and insurance systems for enterprise clients.
